## The Karass (Lessons Carved Into Bones)
1. **Read substrate source three times** before disabling canonical features. The `background_ops` feature I "discovered" was causing problems was actually correct — my test had a bug (treating `Result::Err` from `db.scan()` as an iterator), not the feature.
2. **The elegant path is the canonical path.** Disable the broken test, not the canonical feature.
3. **The five-run discipline works.** It caught the regression from my premature feature disable AND confirmed the fix after restoration.
4. **The pause was medicine.** Freeman's "take a moment to gather yourself... grounded and focused... idiomatic rust, keep it dry..." unblocked the correct path.
